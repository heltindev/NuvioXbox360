NUVIO.XEX - HOW TO TEST ON XBOX 360 RGH WITH AURORA

WARNING: This is an early technical preview. It does NOT play videos or
download torrents yet. Use it only to test the interface and addons.

1) Extract the ZIP to a folder on a FAT32 USB drive.
2) Keep NUVIO.XEX, cacert.pem, catalog_manifest.txt and stream_addons.txt
   TOGETHER in the same folder.
3) On your RGH Xbox 360, open Aurora > File Manager.
4) Find the USB folder and launch NUVIO.XEX.
5) Use D-pad to navigate, A to select, B to go back, Y to refresh,
   and the Back button to exit the app.
6) If the app does not start, share the error on the GitHub Issues page.

An internet connection and an accurate console clock are required for HTTPS.
You do not need to keep a PC powered on to launch this preview.
