NUVIO - XBOX 360 RGH/JTAG TEST GUIDE

STATUS: EXPERIMENTAL. The native XEX compiles with Visual Studio 2010
and XDK 21256, but actual RGH hardware compatibility is not yet verified.
Native FFmpeg/XAudio2 playback is integrated (prefer H.264/AVC up to 720p).

BUILD THE XEX

1. Open VisualStudio2010\NUVIO.sln in Visual Studio 2010.
2. Select Release | Xbox 360 and press F7 (Build Solution).
3. Find VisualStudio2010\Release\NUVIO.xex.
   Do not confuse this with the PC .exe or Debug symbols.

TEST IN XENIA CANARY

1. Open NUVIO.xex with Xenia Canary.
2. Press A on the only login button: "Sign in with QR code".
3. If a QR appears, scan it with your phone and approve via the official
   Nuvio device-link page at https://nuvio.tv/link.
4. If the screen reports a network or RNG error, do NOT bypass the
   security check or use your real Nuvio account in that emulator.
   Some Xenia builds lack secure emulated networking/randomness.

TEST ON AN RGH XBOX 360 WITH AURORA

1. Copy Release\NUVIO.xex into a dedicated folder on FAT32 USB.
2. Open Aurora > File Manager > USB drive.
3. Launch NUVIO.xex.
4. Press A on the QR login button. An internet connection is required.
5. Select a Nuvio cloud profile after approving the device.
   PIN-protected profiles cannot be unlocked yet.
6. Press the Xbox Back button to exit.

No Python process or local PC bridge is required by this native XEX.
Use a compatible network connection and never enter Nuvio passwords on
the Xbox. After the first QR approval, the renewable session is saved locally
as nuvio_session.dat beside the XEX. Keep this folder writable. On the next
launch the app refreshes the saved login instead of asking for a new QR.
Y in Settings signs out and deletes the saved session. Do not share this cache.

Please do not publish this as a completed streaming app. Media playback
needs provider/hardware validation. Native magnet playback is experimental and requires compatible peers and 720p H.264 sources.

PLAY A SOURCE

A on a title opens details; A again opens episodes/sources. Select a source
with A. Magnet/infoHash uses the built-in torrent v1 client unless you set
torrent_server in nuvio.cfg. Choose H.264/AVC up to 720p, preferably AAC stereo.
Known 1080p/4K/HEVC/HDR sources are refused before download.
The sidebar expands with larger buttons and labels when focused.
A/Start pauses; Left/Right seeks 10 seconds; B exits the player.
