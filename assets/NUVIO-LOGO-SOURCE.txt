Official transparent Nuvio logo, downloaded unmodified from:
https://nuvio.tv/assets/Logo_1080x1080.png

Saved as nuvio-logo.png (488 x 536, RGBA PNG).
The source image is embedded by tools/embed_logo.py and decoded by stb_image
at runtime. No internet request is needed to display the app logo.

Reference app and protocol: https://github.com/NuvioMedia/NuvioTV
